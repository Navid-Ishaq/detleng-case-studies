# Power BI as a Data Engineering and Analytics Delivery Platform

## Transforming Raw Business Data into Trusted Analytics Assets

### Executive Summary

Organizations generate large volumes of operational data every day.

Sales transactions, customer activity, inventory movements, financial records, and operational events continuously produce valuable business information.

However, data alone does not create business value.

Before organizations can benefit from reporting, analytics, dashboards, or Business Intelligence, data must be collected, prepared, structured, validated, and transformed into trusted analytical assets.

At DeTLeng, we use a structured Data Engineering methodology to transform raw business data into analytics-ready datasets that support reporting, decision-making, and business growth.

Power BI serves as the implementation platform through which this methodology is delivered.

The result is not simply a dashboard.

The result is a trusted analytics foundation.

---

# The Business Problem

Many organizations face similar challenges:

* Data exists across multiple files and systems.
* Reporting processes are manual and time-consuming.
* KPI calculations are inconsistent.
* Different teams report different numbers.
* Business visibility is limited.
* Decision-making is slowed by data uncertainty.

These challenges are rarely reporting problems.

They are data preparation and analytics problems.

Without reliable data foundations, dashboards cannot be trusted.

# The DeTLeng Approach

Rather than beginning with dashboards, DeTLeng begins with data.

Every engagement follows a structured six-stage Data Engineering methodology:

### 1. Data Extraction

Collect and organize business data from source systems.

---

### 2. Data Cleaning

Improve data quality, consistency, and reliability.

---

### 3. Data Transformation & Integration

Apply business logic and convert technical data into business information.

---

### 4. Data Validation

Verify accuracy and establish trust in analytical outputs.

---

### 5. Analytics Dataset Engineering

Create reporting-ready analytical assets and KPI frameworks.

---

### 6. Delivery & Business Enablement

Provide dashboards, reporting environments, documentation, and decision-support tools.

# Why Power BI?

This enables organizations to move from raw operational data to trusted business intelligence within a single platform.

Power BI supports:

### Data Extraction

Connecting to business systems, spreadsheets, databases, cloud services, and external data sources.

---

### Data Preparation

Cleaning, standardizing, enriching, and transforming business data.

---

### Data Modeling

Creating structured analytical models that support efficient reporting.

---

### KPI Engineering

Developing trusted business metrics and performance indicators.

---

### Business Intelligence

Delivering interactive dashboards, reports, and analytical experiences.
