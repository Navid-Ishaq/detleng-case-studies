# Executive Overview

Data Extraction is the first stage of the Data Engineering lifecycle.

Its purpose is to collect, ingest, and organize raw business data so that it becomes available for downstream processing and analysis.

Without a reliable extraction process, every subsequent stage of the analytics workflow becomes unreliable.

Data quality, reporting accuracy, and business insights all depend on the integrity of the source data being extracted.

For this project, the Online Retail dataset was selected as the source system.

The objective was to ingest the retail transaction data into Power BI while preserving the original structure and content of the source files.

No business transformations were applied during this stage.

The focus was solely on data acquisition and source preservation.

---

# Business Objective

The objective of the extraction phase was to establish a reliable source layer that would serve as the foundation for all future data preparation, analytics engineering, and reporting activities.

Specifically, the extraction process was designed to:

* Collect historical retail transaction data.
* Preserve source integrity.
* Create a repeatable ingestion process.
* Establish a raw data layer.
* Prepare datasets for cleaning and transformation.
* Support future analytical requirements.

At this stage, no reporting logic was introduced.

No KPIs were calculated.

No dashboard structures were created.

The purpose was simply to make business data available for processing.

# Extraction Strategy

The dataset was divided into two historical source periods:

### Dataset 1

retail_raw_2009_2010

### Dataset 2

retail_raw_2010_2011

This approach provides:

* Historical traceability
* Source separation
* Easier validation
* Improved auditability

Each source file was imported independently.

No modifications were made during extraction.

# Key Learning

Data Extraction is not the process of creating insights.

It is the process of establishing a trustworthy source foundation.

Reliable analytics begin with reliable source acquisition.

Every KPI, dashboard, report, and business decision created later in the project ultimately depends on the quality and completeness of the data extracted during this stage.

---

# DeTLeng Principle

The objective of extraction is not to improve data.

The objective of extraction is to preserve data.

Improvement comes later.

Trust begins with preserving the source.
