# DeTLeng Strategic Focus Document

### Version 1.0

### Internal Strategy Document

### Author: Muhammad Naveed Ishaque

# Mission

> **We help organizations transform fragmented business data into trusted reporting, Business Intelligence, and AI-powered decision systems.**

We do not sell technology.

We solve business problems.

---

# Vision

To become a trusted European consultancy specializing in:

* Data Engineering
* Business Intelligence
* AI Assistants
* Intelligent Business Automation

Every solution we build should help businesses make better decisions.

---

# Core Philosophy

Technology is not the product.

Business value is the product.

Data is the foundation.

Knowledge creates intelligence.

AI accelerates everything.

---

# The DeTLeng Product Stack

Only these technologies define the DeTLeng commercial offering.

## Core Skills

✅ BigQuery

✅ ETL / ELT

✅ Power BI / Looker Studio

✅ AI Assistants

✅ MCP Integration

Nothing outside this list is considered a commercial priority unless it directly strengthens one of these capabilities.

# The DeTLeng Asset Model

Every client project must create two outcomes.

## Outcome One

A successful client implementation.

## Outcome Two

A reusable internal asset.

Examples:

* SQL Template
* ETL Template
* BigQuery Framework
* MCP Module
* Documentation Template
* Prompt Library
* Dashboard Component
* AI Workflow

No project should end without increasing the company's intellectual assets.

## Aur akhir mein...

Is document ko padhte hue mujhe ek aur cheez samajh aayi.

Iska title **Strategic Focus Document** hai, lekin asal mein ye tumhare liye **decision filter** hai.

Jab tumhare paas 20 naye ideas aayenge (aur mujhe yaqeen hai aayenge 😄), tum un par debate mat karna.

Sirf is document ke saamne rakhna.

Agar idea DeTLeng ke Product Stack, Client Journey aur Revenue Rules ko mazboot karta hai, us par kaam karo.

Agar nahi karta, to usay "Future Lab" mein rakh do aur aage badh jao.

Mujhe lagta hai ye document tumhare liye kisi business plan se zyada qeemti ho sakta hai, kyun ke tumhari challenge ideas ki kami nahi, **ideas ko filter karna** hai. Ye document usi filter ka pehla version hai.
