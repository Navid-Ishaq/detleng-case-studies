1. Executive Summary

Business Intelligence has evolved significantly over the last decade. While organizations have invested heavily in data warehouses, ETL pipelines, reporting platforms, and dashboards, business users often continue to rely on analysts to answer routine business questions. This dependency creates delays, increases operational overhead, and slows decision-making.

This case study documents how DeTLeng designed and implemented an end-to-end Conversational Business Intelligence platform that combines modern Data Engineering practices with Artificial Intelligence to deliver trusted business insights through natural language.

Instead of beginning with Artificial Intelligence, the implementation started by engineering a robust analytical foundation. The publicly available Brazilian Olist E-Commerce Dataset was imported into Google BigQuery, organized into Raw, Staging, and Analytics layers, and transformed into reusable business models capable of supporting enterprise reporting and analytics.

Once the analytics layer had been fully engineered, a custom DeTLeng MCP Server was developed to provide governed access to approved business intelligence tools. Rather than allowing AI to generate arbitrary SQL queries, the platform exposes carefully designed business functions that retrieve trusted analytical metrics directly from the Analytics Layer.

The conversational experience was built using OpenAI, FastAPI, Google BigQuery, Python, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Business users can ask questions such as:

  • What is our total revenue?
  • How many customers do we have?
  • Which products generate the highest sales?
  • How is delivery performance changing over time?
  • Which states contribute the most revenue?

Every response is generated from trusted analytical datasets rather than model assumptions, ensuring consistency, governance, and business accuracy.

The completed solution demonstrates the engineering philosophy of DeTLeng:

We do not build chatbots and connect them to data. We first engineer trusted Data Engineering foundations, then extend them with Artificial Intelligence to create Intelligent Business Systems.

This implementation is more than a technical demonstration. It represents DeTLeng's approach to transforming raw business data into governed analytics and ultimately into conversational decision support for business managers, analysts, and enterprise stakeholders.

2. Project Overview

This case study documents the complete engineering journey undertaken by DeTLeng to transform a publicly available e-commerce dataset into a production-style Conversational Business Intelligence platform. Unlike traditional demonstrations that begin with dashboards or AI chatbots, this implementation started with understanding the business behind the data. Every table was investigated, validated, transformed, and modeled before any Artificial Intelligence components were introduced.

The objective was not simply to build another chatbot. The objective was to demonstrate how modern Data Engineering, Analytics Engineering, Business Intelligence, and Artificial Intelligence can work together to create an Intelligent Business System capable of answering business questions using trusted enterprise data.

Project Dataset

The implementation uses the publicly available Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist, one of the most widely used real-world datasets for analytics, Data Engineering, and Business Intelligence projects.

Dataset Name
Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist

Source
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce

About the Dataset

The dataset contains historical Brazilian e-commerce transactions collected between 2016 and 2018 and consists of nine interconnected CSV files representing different business domains. These include:

  • Customers
  • Orders
  • Order Items
  • Products
  • Sellers
  • Payments
  • Customer Reviews
  • Geolocation
  • Product Categories

Although these files are publicly available, they do not represent an analytics-ready solution. They require significant engineering effort to understand relationships, validate data quality, standardize schemas, and design analytical models suitable for Business Intelligence.

Engineering Philosophy

Before writing SQL transformations or designing dashboards, DeTLeng performed detailed business and technical investigations for every major dataset. Each table was analyzed to understand:

  • Business purpose
  • Column definitions
  • Relationships
  • Primary keys
  • Data quality
  • Business risks
  • Transformation requirements
  • Analytical value

These investigations formed the foundation for building the Raw Layer, Staging Layer, Analytics Layer, and ultimately the Conversational Business Intelligence platform documented throughout this case study.

At DeTLeng, Data Engineering begins with understanding the business behind the data—not simply importing CSV files into a warehouse.

3. Business Challenge

Modern organizations generate enormous amounts of business data every day. Orders, customers, products, payments, deliveries, and customer reviews are continuously collected and stored across operational systems. While this data represents one of the organization's most valuable assets, transforming it into reliable business intelligence remains a significant challenge.

Most companies already have dashboards, reports, and Business Intelligence platforms. However, business managers still depend heavily on analysts to answer simple operational questions. Every new business question often results in another SQL query, another dashboard modification, or another reporting request.

Traditional Workflow

Business Question
↓
Email or Message the Analyst
↓
SQL Query Development
↓
Dashboard or Report
↓
Business Decision

Although this workflow has served organizations for many years, it introduces delays, increases dependence on technical teams, and prevents decision-makers from interacting directly with trusted business data.

The Engineering Challenge

The objective of this project was never to build another dashboard or a generic AI chatbot. The real engineering challenge was significantly larger:

  • Understand an unfamiliar real-world business dataset.
  • Investigate relationships across nine independent CSV files.
  • Import and organize the data inside Google BigQuery.
  • Design a production-style Raw, Staging, and Analytics architecture.
  • Create reusable analytical models instead of one-off SQL queries.
  • Develop governed Business Intelligence tools.
  • Build a secure MCP (Model Context Protocol) server.
  • Integrate OpenAI with trusted analytical data instead of raw tables.
  • Deploy the complete solution to a production environment.

Why Data Engineering Came First

One of the most important architectural decisions made during this implementation was to postpone Artificial Intelligence until a trusted analytical foundation had been established.

Instead of immediately connecting an AI model to raw business data, the project first focused on engineering a complete Data Engineering workflow consisting of:

Raw Data
↓
Raw Layer
↓
Staging Layer
↓
Analytics Layer
↓
Business Intelligence Models
↓
DeTLeng MCP Server
↓
OpenAI
↓
Conversational Business Intelligence

This sequence ensured that every answer generated by the AI assistant originated from curated analytical models rather than directly from operational data.

Production Challenges

Beyond Data Engineering, the implementation involved several production-level engineering challenges that required careful design and troubleshooting throughout the project.

  • Designing scalable BigQuery datasets.
  • Creating reusable SQL transformation models.
  • Managing Google Cloud IAM permissions and Service Accounts.
  • Authenticating BigQuery access securely.
  • Building and testing the DeTLeng MCP Server.
  • Developing Business Intelligence tools using Python.
  • Integrating OpenAI with FastAPI through MCP.
  • Managing environment variables and API security.
  • Deploying the backend to Render.
  • Connecting the production chatbot with live analytical datasets.
  • Testing business responses for consistency and accuracy.

Every challenge contributed to the final objective: enabling business users to ask questions in natural language while receiving trusted answers generated directly from governed analytical data.

At DeTLeng, Artificial Intelligence is never the starting point. Trusted Data Engineering comes first. Artificial Intelligence extends that foundation.
