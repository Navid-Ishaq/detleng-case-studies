Executive Summary

This case study documents the complete implementation of an AI-powered Conversational Business Intelligence platform developed by DeTLeng. The project demonstrates how a real-world e-commerce dataset can be transformed into a production-ready analytics solution by combining Data Engineering, Business Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence, and cloud technologies.

Rather than building another dashboard, the objective was to enable business users to ask questions in natural language while receiving trusted answers generated directly from analytical business models stored in Google BigQuery. The implementation follows a layered Data Engineering approach consisting of Raw, Staging, and Analytics layers, followed by a custom-built DeTLeng MCP Server, FastAPI backend, OpenAI integration, and a live AI chatbot deployed on the web.

This project reflects DeTLeng's engineering philosophy: Artificial Intelligence becomes truly valuable only when it is built upon trusted Data Engineering.

Project Overview

Dataset Name
Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist
Source URL
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce

The project began with the publicly available Brazilian Olist E-Commerce Dataset, which contains nine CSV files representing customers, orders, products, payments, sellers, reviews, and geographical information. Instead of using the dataset directly for reporting, the complete Data Engineering lifecycle was implemented.

The CSV files were imported into Google BigQuery, organized into Raw, Staging, and Analytics layers, and transformed into reusable business models using SQL. Once the Analytics Layer was complete, a custom DeTLeng MCP Server was developed to expose approved Business Intelligence tools. A FastAPI backend and OpenAI integration were then added, allowing users to interact with the analytics layer through a conversational AI chatbot.

The final solution demonstrates how enterprise data can be transformed into an Intelligent Business System that combines trusted analytics with natural language interaction.

1. Executive Summary

Business Intelligence has evolved significantly over the last decade. While organizations have invested heavily in data warehouses, ETL pipelines, reporting platforms, and dashboards, business users often continue to rely on analysts to answer routine business questions. This dependency creates delays, increases operational overhead, and slows decision-making.

This case study documents how DeTLeng designed and implemented an end-to-end Conversational Business Intelligence platform that combines modern Data Engineering practices with Artificial Intelligence to deliver trusted business insights through natural language.

Instead of beginning with Artificial Intelligence, the implementation started by engineering a robust analytical foundation. The publicly available Brazilian Olist E-Commerce Dataset was imported into Google BigQuery, organized into Raw, Staging, and Analytics layers, and transformed into reusable business models capable of supporting enterprise reporting and analytics.

Once the analytics layer had been fully engineered, a custom DeTLeng MCP Server was developed to provide governed access to approved business intelligence tools. Rather than allowing AI to generate arbitrary SQL queries, the platform exposes carefully designed business functions that retrieve trusted analytical metrics directly from the Analytics Layer.

The conversational experience was built using OpenAI, FastAPI, Google BigQuery, Python, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Business users can ask questions such as:

  • What is our total revenue?
  • How many customers do we have?
  • Which products generate the highest sales?
  • How is delivery performance changing over time?
  • Which states contribute the most revenue?

Every response is generated from trusted analytical datasets rather than model assumptions, ensuring consistency, governance, and business accuracy.

The completed solution demonstrates the engineering philosophy of DeTLeng:

We do not build chatbots and connect them to data. We first engineer trusted Data Engineering foundations, then extend them with Artificial Intelligence to create Intelligent Business Systems.

This implementation is more than a technical demonstration. It represents DeTLeng's approach to transforming raw business data into governed analytics and ultimately into conversational decision support for business managers, analysts, and enterprise stakeholders.

2. Project Overview

This case study documents the complete engineering journey undertaken by DeTLeng to transform a publicly available e-commerce dataset into a production-style Conversational Business Intelligence platform. Unlike traditional demonstrations that begin with dashboards or AI chatbots, this implementation started with understanding the business behind the data. Every table was investigated, validated, transformed, and modeled before any Artificial Intelligence components were introduced.

The objective was not simply to build another chatbot. The objective was to demonstrate how modern Data Engineering, Analytics Engineering, Business Intelligence, and Artificial Intelligence can work together to create an Intelligent Business System capable of answering business questions using trusted enterprise data.

Project Dataset

The implementation uses the publicly available Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist, one of the most widely used real-world datasets for analytics, Data Engineering, and Business Intelligence projects.

Dataset Name
Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist

Source

About the Dataset

The dataset contains historical Brazilian e-commerce transactions collected between 2016 and 2018 and consists of nine interconnected CSV files representing different business domains. These include:

  • Customers
  • Orders
  • Order Items
  • Products
  • Sellers
  • Payments
  • Customer Reviews
  • Geolocation
  • Product Categories

Although these files are publicly available, they do not represent an analytics-ready solution. They require significant engineering effort to understand relationships, validate data quality, standardize schemas, and design analytical models suitable for Business Intelligence.

Engineering Philosophy

Before writing SQL transformations or designing dashboards, DeTLeng performed detailed business and technical investigations for every major dataset. Each table was analyzed to understand:

  • Business purpose
  • Column definitions
  • Relationships
  • Primary keys
  • Data quality
  • Business risks
  • Transformation requirements
  • Analytical value

These investigations formed the foundation for building the Raw Layer, Staging Layer, Analytics Layer, and ultimately the Conversational Business Intelligence platform documented throughout this case study.

At DeTLeng, Data Engineering begins with understanding the business behind the data—not simply importing CSV files into a warehouse.

Key Takeaway

This project was designed to demonstrate an end-to-end engineering workflow, starting with raw business data and ending with an AI-powered conversational analytics platform. Every layer of the architecture was intentionally built before introducing Artificial Intelligence, reinforcing DeTLeng's philosophy that trustworthy AI depends on trustworthy Data Engineering.

6. Solution Architecture

Building a conversational Business Intelligence platform requires much more than connecting an AI model to a database. A reliable solution must begin with trusted data, follow disciplined Data Engineering practices, and expose only governed analytical models to Artificial Intelligence.

Throughout this implementation, DeTLeng deliberately adopted a layered architecture instead of allowing the AI assistant to interact directly with raw business data. This architectural decision ensured security, consistency, scalability, and reliable business responses.

Rather than treating Artificial Intelligence as the foundation, the implementation follows the DeTLeng Intelligent Business Systems Framework, where Data Engineering serves as the foundation and AI extends that foundation to support business decision-making.

Architecture Philosophy

Raw Business Data
↓
Data Engineering
↓

↓
Business Intelligence
↓
Artificial Intelligence
↓

Every layer of the architecture has a clearly defined responsibility. Business users never interact with raw operational tables. Instead, every question flows through governed analytical models before reaching the AI assistant.

Core Architectural Components

  • Public Brazilian Olist E-Commerce Dataset
  • Google BigQuery Cloud Data Warehouse
  • Raw Data Layer
  • Staging Layer for Data Transformation
  • Analytics Layer using Fact and Dimension Models
  • Business Intelligence SQL Models
  • DeTLeng MCP Server
  • Business Intelligence Tool Library
  • FastAPI Backend
  • OpenAI Integration
  • Conversational AI Business Assistant

Why This Architecture Matters

Many AI implementations connect Large Language Models directly to operational databases. While this may appear simple, it introduces significant risks including incorrect SQL generation, inconsistent business metrics, excessive cloud costs, and security concerns.

To avoid these risks, DeTLeng implemented an intermediary Business Intelligence layer through a custom-built MCP Server. Instead of generating SQL dynamically, the AI assistant invokes predefined Business Intelligence tools that execute validated analytical queries against the Analytics Layer only.

  • Reusable Business Intelligence tools.
