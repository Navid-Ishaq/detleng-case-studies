# DeTLeng | Data Engineering, ETL & Analytics Solutions

### Transforming Raw Business Data into Analytics-Ready Assets

Many reporting challenges are not dashboard problems.

They are data problems.

Organizations often struggle with disconnected data sources, inconsistent KPI calculations, manual reporting processes, duplicate records, and datasets that are difficult to trust.

At DeTLeng, we help businesses transform raw, fragmented, and inconsistent data into clean, structured, analytics-ready datasets that support reliable reporting, business intelligence, and decision-making.

Our focus is not simply creating dashboards.

Our focus is building the data foundation that makes reporting trustworthy, scalable, and maintainable.

## Core Services

### Data Engineering

Designing structured data pipelines and workflows that transform raw operational data into analytics-ready assets.

### ETL Development

Building repeatable Extract, Transform, and Load processes that standardize, cleanse, and prepare data for reporting and analysis.

### BigQuery Solutions

Developing cloud-based analytical datasets, reporting layers, and scalable warehouse structures using Google BigQuery.

### Analytics Engineering

Bridging the gap between raw data and business reporting through dimensional modeling, business logic implementation, and analytics layer design.

### Reporting Dataset Preparation

Creating trusted datasets optimized for Power BI, Looker Studio, executive reporting, and KPI dashboards.

### Data Quality & Validation

Improving confidence in reporting through structured validation, standardization, and business-rule enforcement.
