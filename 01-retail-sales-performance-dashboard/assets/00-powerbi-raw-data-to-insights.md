# DeTLeng Data Engineering Methodology Using Power BI

## An End-to-End Approach to Building Analytics-Ready Business Data

### Introduction

Data Engineering is often associated with cloud platforms, data warehouses, and large-scale enterprise systems.

However, the core principles of Data Engineering remain the same regardless of the technology stack being used.

At DeTLeng, we define Data Engineering as the process of transforming raw business data into trusted, analytics-ready assets that support reporting, analysis, and business decision-making.

Whether the implementation platform is Power BI, BigQuery, SQL Server, Snowflake, or another analytics technology, the methodology remains consistent.

This case study demonstrates a complete end-to-end Data Engineering implementation using Microsoft Power BI and the publicly available Online Retail dataset.

The project showcases how raw transactional data can be transformed into structured business information through a disciplined Data Engineering process.

# Data Engineering Lifecycle

The project follows the DeTLeng Six-Step Data Engineering Methodology.

## Step 1 — Data Extraction

Collect and ingest raw business data from source systems.

Objective:

Move source data into the analytics environment.

---

## Step 2 — Data Cleaning

Improve data quality and consistency.

Objective:

Create trusted datasets by removing errors, duplicates, and inconsistencies.

---

## Step 3 — Data Transformation & Integration

Apply business logic and prepare analytical structures.

Objective:

Convert technical data into business information.

---

## Step 4 — Data Validation

Verify accuracy and establish trust.

Objective:

Ensure metrics, calculations, and datasets accurately reflect business activity.

---

## Step 5 — Analytics Dataset Engineering

Build reporting-ready structures.

Objective:

Create optimized analytical datasets that support reporting and Business Intelligence.

---

## Step 6 — Delivery & Business Enablement

Deliver business value through reporting, documentation, and stakeholder enablement.

Objective:

Provide trusted analytics assets that support ongoing decision-making.

---

# Key Principle

The dashboard is not the project.

The dashboard is the final consumer of the Data Engineering process.

The true value is created through:

* Data Extraction
* Data Quality Engineering
* Data Transformation
* Validation
* Analytics Dataset Design
* Business Enablement

The dashboard simply makes the results visible.
