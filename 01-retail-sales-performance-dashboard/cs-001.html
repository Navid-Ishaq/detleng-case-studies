Case Study #01

Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

End-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed in Power BI to transform raw retail transaction data into actionable insights for sales monitoring, customer analysis, and executive decision-making.

📌 Executive Summary

This project demonstrates the complete development lifecycle of a Retail Sales Performance Dashboard using Power BI. The solution converts over 500,000 retail transactions into interactive analytics that help stakeholders monitor performance, identify trends, and support strategic business decisions.

🎯 Business Objective

Retail organizations generate thousands of transactions daily. Without centralized reporting, it becomes challenging to evaluate sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and revenue growth.

Project Goals

  • Track overall sales performance
  • Analyze customer purchasing behavior
  • Monitor product profitability
  • Identify revenue trends and seasonality
  • Deliver executive-level KPI reporting

⚙️ Data Preparation & ETL

Data transformation and cleansing were performed using Power Query to ensure reporting accuracy and consistency.

  1. Import raw retail dataset
  2. Remove null and invalid records
  3. Exclude cancelled transactions
  4. Standardize data types
  5. Create calendar attributes
  6. Generate Month, Quarter, and Year dimensions
  7. Create Revenue calculations
  8. Prepare analytical model for reporting

🗄️ Data Modeling

A star-schema-inspired data model was designed to optimize report performance and analytical flexibility.

Fact Table

  • Sales Transactions

Dimension Tables

  • Date
  • Product
  • Customer
  • Country

DISTINCTCOUNT(StockCode)

📈 Dashboard & Reporting

The final dashboard provides an executive-level view of retail performance through interactive visualizations and KPI monitoring.

  • KPI Cards
  • Revenue Trend Analysis
  • Quarterly Performance Analysis
  • Revenue by Country
  • Top Products by Revenue
  • Interactive Slicers & Filters

💡 Key Insights

  • Revenue reached its highest levels during Q4.
  • Strong seasonal purchasing patterns were identified.
  • A limited number of products generated a majority of revenue.
  • Customer activity was concentrated within specific geographic regions.

🚀 Business Impact

  • Improved sales visibility across the organization
  • Faster reporting and decision-making
  • Identification of growth opportunities
  • Enhanced executive reporting capabilities
