📂 Step 1 — Source Data Acquisition
The project started with the Online Retail Dataset obtained from the UCI Machine Learning Repository.

The source workbook contained two separate worksheets covering different reporting periods:

Year 2009-2010
Year 2010-2011
Combined, these worksheets contained more than one million retail transaction records, including invoices, products, quantities, pricing information, customer identifiers, and country-level sales information.

The objective was to consolidate these datasets into a single analytical model capable of supporting executive reporting and business analysis.

Excellent! Error se exactly pata chal gaya problem kya hai. 👍

BigQuery ne **InvoiceDate** ko automatically **TIMESTAMP** samajh liya hai, lekin tumhari CSV mein date format hai:

```text
13/12/2009 09:58
DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM
```

```text
2009-12-13 09:58:00
YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS
```

Is liye upload fail ho raha hai.

### Next Step (Recommended)

Now create these analytics queries:

#### Revenue by Country

```sql
SELECT
Country,
ROUND(SUM(Revenue),2) AS Revenue
FROM detleng_retail.retail_staging
WHERE Revenue > 0
GROUP BY Country
ORDER BY Revenue DESC;
```

#### Revenue by Year

```sql
SELECT
EXTRACT(YEAR FROM InvoiceDate) AS Year,
ROUND(SUM(Revenue),2) AS Revenue
FROM detleng_retail.retail_staging
WHERE Revenue > 0
GROUP BY Year
ORDER BY Year;
```

#### Revenue by Month

```sql
SELECT
FORMAT_DATE('%B', DATE(InvoiceDate)) AS MonthName,
ROUND(SUM(Revenue),2) AS Revenue
FROM detleng_retail.retail_staging
WHERE Revenue > 0
GROUP BY MonthName
ORDER BY Revenue DESC;
```
