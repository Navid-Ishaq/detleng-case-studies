Most people would create:


and stop.

You can do something much smarter.

One dataset can generate:

```text
Business Case Study
+
Data Engineering Case Study
+
BigQuery Case Study
+
ETL Case Study
+
Power BI Case Study
+
Blogger Articles
```

Example:

Retail Dataset

produces:

```text
CS-001 Retail Dashboard

CS-002 Retail Analytics Dataset

AR-001 Dashboard Design Lessons

AR-002 BigQuery ETL Lessons

AR-003 KPI Design Lessons
```

One project becomes multiple assets.

That's exactly how consulting firms maximize the value of implementation work.

```text
15 Case Studies
```

and build everything around that foundation. That's enough content to establish a very credible Data Engineering and Analytics consulting presence.
