# Executive Summary

As part of the DeTLeng E-Commerce Analytics initiative, a comprehensive investigation was conducted on the Orders table within the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

The Orders table represents the operational core of the e-commerce ecosystem. Every customer purchase, fulfillment event, delivery milestone, and order status transition ultimately flows through this dataset.

While many analytics projects begin with dashboards and visualizations, effective Data Engineering begins with understanding the operational processes hidden inside the data.

The objective of this postmortem was to assess:

* Data quality
* Operational performance
* Delivery efficiency
* Order lifecycle behavior
* Business intelligence opportunities

The analysis revealed a generally healthy operational environment characterized by high delivery completion rates, low cancellation rates, and strong data integrity. However, several opportunities for operational optimization were identified, particularly around delivery performance and order processing efficiency.

# Data Quality Assessment

Before extracting business insights, the integrity of the dataset was validated.

## Duplicate Order Analysis

Result:

```text
0 Duplicate Orders
```

### Observation

The absence of duplicate order identifiers is an important indicator of dataset reliability.

Duplicate transaction records can significantly distort:

* Revenue calculations
* Order counts
* Customer metrics
* Executive dashboards

### Business Impact

This result provides confidence that future KPI calculations built upon this dataset are unlikely to suffer from transaction inflation.

---

## Missing Order Identifier Analysis

Result:

```text
0 Missing Order IDs
```

### Observation

Every order record contains a valid business identifier.

### Business Impact

This ensures:

* Full traceability
* Reliable auditing
* Consistent downstream joins
* Accurate relationship mapping

This is particularly important when integrating Orders with Customers, Payments, Reviews, Products, and Sellers.

# Order Approval Performance

## Average Approval Time

Result:

```text
10.06 Hours
```

---

## Interpretation

This metric measures the average time between:

```text
Order Placement
→
Order Approval
```

A delay of approximately 10 hours may not appear significant, but in modern digital commerce environments, approval speed directly affects customer expectations and fulfillment efficiency.

---

## Business Implications

Long approval cycles can create:

* Fulfillment delays
* Increased customer uncertainty
* Slower shipping initiation
* Longer delivery times

---

## Improvement Opportunity

Organizations may explore:

* Automated payment verification
* Fraud detection optimization
* Real-time approval workflows

Reducing approval times can accelerate the entire fulfillment process.
