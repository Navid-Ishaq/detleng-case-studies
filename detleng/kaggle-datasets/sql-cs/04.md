# Executive Summary

As part of the DeTLeng E-Commerce Analytics Program, a comprehensive postmortem investigation was conducted on the Customers table within the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset.

The objective of this analysis was to evaluate customer population characteristics, geographic distribution, data quality, market concentration, and potential business expansion opportunities.

While transactional datasets explain what customers purchased, the customer master dataset explains who the customers are and where they are located.

Understanding customer geography is fundamental to:

* Market expansion strategy
* Customer acquisition planning
* Revenue forecasting
* Logistics optimization
* Regional marketing investments
* Customer analytics initiatives

The investigation revealed a well-structured customer dataset with strong data integrity, evidence of repeat purchasing behavior, significant customer concentration within key Brazilian states, and multiple opportunities for future market growth analysis.

## Customer Uniqueness Assessment

### Result

```text
96,096 Unique Customers
```

### Observation

The number of unique customers is slightly lower than the total number of customer records.

Difference:

```text
99,441 - 96,096
=
3,345
```

---

## Business Interpretation

This result is particularly important.

The difference does not indicate duplicate data.

Instead, it reflects repeat customer activity.

Within the Olist dataset:

### customer_id

Represents an order-specific customer reference.

### customer_unique_id

Represents the actual customer identity.

This means a portion of customers returned to make additional purchases.

---

## Strategic Insight

One of the most expensive activities in commerce is acquiring new customers.

Research consistently shows that customer retention is often significantly less expensive than customer acquisition.

Source:

Harvard Business Review

https://hbr.org/2014/10/the-value-of-keeping-the-right-customers

The presence of repeat customers suggests:

* Existing customer trust
* Marketplace engagement
* Potential customer loyalty

Although the dataset does not yet provide a complete retention analysis, the results indicate that repeat purchasing behavior exists and warrants deeper investigation in future case studies.

## Strategic Interpretation

This is the most important finding of the entire investigation.

The business demonstrates significant dependence on the São Paulo market.

While this concentration may indicate strong market penetration, it also introduces concentration risk.

---

## Business Risk

Organizations heavily dependent upon a single geographic market may be exposed to:

* Economic downturns
* Competitive disruption
* Regional logistics issues
* Regulatory changes
* Market saturation

If customer acquisition slows in São Paulo, overall business growth may be affected.

---

## Executive Recommendation

Protect existing São Paulo market share while gradually diversifying customer acquisition efforts into secondary growth markets.

Priority candidates include:

* Rio de Janeiro
* Minas Gerais
* Paraná
* Rio Grande do Sul
* Santa Catarina

These regions represent an interesting analytical opportunity.

# DeTLeng Perspective

Transactions tell us what happened.

Customers tell us who made it happen.

The Customer table represents far more than a collection of identifiers and locations. It provides the foundation for understanding market reach, customer behavior, regional demand, and future growth opportunities.

Without customer understanding, businesses can measure revenue.

With customer understanding, businesses can explain revenue.

And once revenue can be explained, it can be improved.

---

## Final Thought

Every successful analytics platform begins with understanding the customer.

Products generate sales.

Orders generate transactions.

But customers generate businesses.

Understanding where customers are, how they behave, and how they contribute to growth is one of the most valuable investments any organization can make in its analytics journey.
