# Executive Summary

Following the successful completion of Orders and Customers postmortem investigations, the next phase of the DeTLeng Operation Theatre focused on analyzing the Product and Seller ecosystem.

This investigation aims to answer several critical business questions:

* What products drive marketplace activity?
* Which categories generate the strongest demand?

# Diagnostic Query 1

## Total Order Items Processed

### SQL

```sql
SELECT COUNT(*) AS total_order_items
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_order_items`;
```

### Result

112,650 Order Items

### Business Insight

The marketplace processed more than 112 thousand individual product line items.

This number exceeds total orders because a single customer order may contain multiple products.

This confirms:

* Multi-item purchasing behavior
* Basket-building activity
* Product bundling opportunities

### Executive Observation

Higher order-item volume compared to total order volume indicates healthy product diversity and customer engagement.

# Diagnostic Query 7

## Top Selling Product Categories

### SQL

```sql
SELECT
p.product_category_name,
COUNT(*) AS total_sales
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_order_items` oi
JOIN `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_products` p
ON oi.product_id = p.product_id
GROUP BY p.product_category_name
ORDER BY total_sales DESC;
```

### Key Results

| Category               |  Sales |
| ---------------------- | -----: |
| cama_mesa_banho        | 11,115 |
| beleza_saude           |  9,670 |
| esporte_lazer          |  8,641 |
| moveis_decoracao       |  8,334 |
| informatica_acessorios |  7,827 |

### Business Interpretation

The strongest demand exists within:

* Home & Living
* Health & Beauty
* Sports & Lifestyle
* Furniture
* Consumer Technology

### Executive Insight

Customer purchasing behavior strongly favors lifestyle-enhancing products.

The marketplace benefits from diversified category demand, reducing commercial concentration risk.

# Diagnostic Query 8

## Seller Geography Analysis

### SQL

```sql
SELECT
seller_city,
seller_state,
COUNT(*) AS total_items_sold
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_order_items` oi
JOIN `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_sellers` s
ON oi.seller_id = s.seller_id
GROUP BY seller_city,seller_state
ORDER BY total_items_sold DESC;
```

### Key Results

| Seller City           | State | Items Sold |
| --------------------- | ----- | ---------: |
| Sao Paulo             | SP    |     27,983 |
| Ibitinga              | SP    |      7,750 |
| Santo Andre           | SP    |      2,964 |
| Curitiba              | PR    |      2,944 |
| Sao Jose do Rio Preto | SP    |      2,579 |

### Business Interpretation

The marketplace supply chain is heavily concentrated in São Paulo State.

### Executive Observation

This concentration creates:

Advantages:

* Faster deliveries
* Strong logistics networks
* Lower fulfillment complexity

Risks:

* Regional dependency
* Supply concentration exposure
* Operational vulnerability during disruptions

# Strategic Business Conclusions

The Product and Seller investigation reveals a mature and highly diversified marketplace ecosystem.

Key findings include:

### Marketplace Scale

* 112,650 order items processed
* 32,951 unique products sold
* 3,095 active sellers

### Commercial Performance

* Total revenue exceeds 13.5 million
* Average product value approximately 121
* Logistics costs exceed 2.25 million

### Customer Demand

Strongest categories:

* Home & Living
* Health & Beauty
* Sports & Leisure
* Furniture
* Technology

### Supply Chain Structure

Seller activity is heavily concentrated in São Paulo State, confirming its role as the primary commercial and logistics hub of the marketplace.

---

# DeTLeng Executive Takeaway

The marketplace demonstrates strong demand diversification, broad product coverage, and a substantial seller ecosystem. However, seller concentration within a limited geographic region introduces long-term operational risk that should be monitored as the marketplace scales.

From a Business Intelligence perspective, the ecosystem appears commercially healthy, operationally efficient, and well-positioned for future analytical modeling, profitability analysis, seller performance monitoring, and executive dashboard development.
