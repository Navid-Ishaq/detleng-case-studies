# Executive Overview

Following the successful completion of the Orders Table Postmortem and Customers Table Postmortem, the next stage of the DeTLeng E-Commerce Analytics Initiative focuses on the Order Items dataset.

While the Orders table provides visibility into transaction events and customer purchasing activity, the Order Items table represents the commercial heart of the e-commerce operation. Every unit sold, every product purchased, every seller transaction, and every revenue-generating event ultimately originates from this dataset.

From a business perspective, the Order Items table answers one of the most important executive questions:

> What is actually generating revenue?

This investigation serves as the foundation for future Product Analytics, Seller Analytics, Revenue Analytics, and Executive Business Intelligence reporting.

# Investigation Series

## Diagnostic Query 1

### Total Order Items

### Business Question

How many individual line items exist across all orders?

### SQL

```sql
SELECT COUNT(*) AS total_order_items
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_order_items`;
```

### Expected Insight

Measures the total sales volume processed by the platform.

### Business Value

Provides the first indication of transaction density and purchasing activity.

---

## Diagnostic Query 2

### Total Unique Products Sold

### Business Question

How many distinct products have been purchased?

### SQL

```sql
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT product_id)
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_order_items`;
```

### Expected Insight

Determines the breadth of the product portfolio.

### Business Value

Supports assortment planning, catalog optimization, and inventory strategy.

## Diagnostic Query 3

### Expected Insight

## Diagnostic Query 4

### Revenue Analysis

### Business Question

What is the total value of products sold?

### SQL

```sql
SELECT
ROUND(SUM(price),2) AS total_revenue
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_order_items`;
```

### Expected Insight

Calculates gross merchandise value (GMV).

### Business Value

Provides one of the most critical executive KPIs for commercial performance measurement.

---

## Diagnostic Query 5

### Freight Cost Analysis

### Business Question

What is the total logistics cost associated with customer deliveries?

### SQL

```sql
SELECT
ROUND(SUM(freight_value),2) AS total_freight
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_order_items`;
```

### Expected Insight

Quantifies transportation and shipping costs.

### Business Value

Supports margin analysis and logistics optimization initiatives.

---

## Diagnostic Query 6

### Average Item Price

### Business Question

What is the average selling price of products?

### SQL

```sql
SELECT
ROUND(AVG(price),2) AS avg_item_price
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_order_items`;
```

### Expected Insight

Establishes average transaction pricing behavior.

### Business Value

Supports pricing strategy, market positioning, and customer spending analysis.

---

## Diagnostic Query 7

### Top Selling Products

### Business Question

Which products are purchased most frequently?

### SQL

```sql
SELECT
product_id,
COUNT(*) AS total_sales
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_order_items`
GROUP BY product_id
ORDER BY total_sales DESC
LIMIT 20;
```

### Expected Insight

Identifies product demand leaders.

### Business Value

Supports inventory planning, product prioritization, and promotional strategies.

---

## Diagnostic Query 8

### Top Performing Sellers

### Business Question

Which sellers contribute the largest volume of sales activity?

### SQL

```sql
SELECT
seller_id,
COUNT(*) AS total_items_sold
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_order_items`
GROUP BY seller_id
ORDER BY total_items_sold DESC
LIMIT 20;
```

### Expected Insight

Highlights marketplace leaders.

### Business Value

Supports seller performance management and strategic partnership decisions.
