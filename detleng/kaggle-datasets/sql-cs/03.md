## Investigation 1

### Total Customer Records

Business Question

How many customer records are stored in the source system?

Diagnostic SQL

```sql
SELECT COUNT(*) AS total_customers
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_customers`;
```

Expected Outcome

Establishes customer population size.

Business Relevance

Provides the baseline for all future customer analytics.

---

## Investigation 2

### Unique Customer Assessment

Business Question

How many unique customers exist?

Diagnostic SQL

```sql
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT customer_unique_id)
AS unique_customers
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_customers`;
```

Expected Outcome

Determines actual customer population.

Business Relevance

Required for customer growth, retention, and lifetime value analysis.

---

## Investigation 3

### Duplicate Customer Validation

Business Question

Does the dataset contain duplicate customer records?

Diagnostic SQL

```sql
SELECT
COUNT(*) -
COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)
AS duplicate_records
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_customers`;
```

Expected Outcome

Measures duplicate customer presence.

Business Relevance

Duplicate customers distort executive reporting and KPI accuracy.

---

## Investigation 4

### State-Level Customer Distribution

Business Question

Which states contribute the largest customer base?

Diagnostic SQL

```sql
SELECT
customer_state,
COUNT(*) AS total_customers
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_customers`
GROUP BY customer_state
ORDER BY total_customers DESC;
```

Expected Outcome

State-wise customer rankings.

Business Relevance

Supports regional sales planning and resource allocation.

---

## Investigation 5

### City-Level Customer Distribution

Business Question

Which cities generate the highest customer concentration?

Diagnostic SQL

```sql
SELECT
customer_city,
COUNT(*) AS total_customers
FROM `detleng-case-studies.cs003_olist_raw.cs003_olist_raw_customers`
GROUP BY customer_city
ORDER BY total_customers DESC
LIMIT 20;
```

Expected Outcome

Top customer cities.

Business Relevance

Supports local market targeting and demand forecasting.
