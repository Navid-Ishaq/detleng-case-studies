# Strategic Positioning

At DeTLeng, we believe that successful analytics projects begin long before a dashboard is created.

Many organizations focus on reporting tools as the starting point of Business Intelligence initiatives.

However, dashboards are only the final presentation layer.

The true value is created through disciplined Data Engineering practices that transform raw business data into trusted analytical assets.

Our approach combines the strengths of Google BigQuery and Power BI to deliver complete end-to-end analytics solutions.

---

# The DeTLeng Philosophy

We do not start with dashboards.

We start with data.

Raw business data is often fragmented, inconsistent, incomplete, and difficult to analyze.

Before meaningful reporting can occur, data must be:

* Collected
* Organized
* Cleaned
* Standardized
* Validated
* Structured

Only after these foundations are established can reliable reporting and decision-making occur.

This philosophy is reflected in the DeTLeng Data Engineering Methodology.

# Why BigQuery?

BigQuery serves as the Data Engineering platform within the DeTLeng architecture.

Its role is not visualization.

Its role is engineering.

BigQuery provides a scalable environment for:

* Data Storage
* SQL Analytics
* ETL Development
* Data Transformation
* Data Validation
* Analytics Dataset Preparation
* Data Warehousing

This is where raw business information is transformed into structured analytical assets.

---

# Why Power BI?

Power BI serves as the Business Intelligence delivery platform.

While BigQuery engineers the data, Power BI communicates the results.

Power BI provides:

* Interactive Dashboards
* Executive Reporting
* KPI Monitoring
* Trend Analysis
* Self-Service Analytics

This is where business users interact with the information prepared during the Data Engineering process.

# Understanding the Relationship Between BigQuery and Power BI

One of the most important concepts within the DeTLeng framework is understanding the distinct role of each platform.

### BigQuery

Makes relationships executable.

Using SQL, BigQuery connects business entities dynamically and prepares analytical outputs.

---

### Power BI

Makes relationships visible.

Using analytical models and visual interfaces, Power BI allows business users to explore and understand those relationships.
