# From Raw Business Data to Analytics-Ready Datasets

## How DeTLeng Transforms Business Data into Trusted Analytics Assets

Most organizations generate large volumes of data every day.

Sales systems record transactions. Websites collect visitor activity. CRMs store customer interactions. Accounting platforms manage financial records. Marketing tools track campaigns and conversions.

While data is abundant, it is often fragmented, inconsistent, and difficult to use for reliable reporting and decision-making.

At DeTLeng, we help organizations transform raw business data into structured, validated, and analytics-ready datasets that support Business Intelligence, reporting, automation, and strategic decision-making.

Our process follows a structured Data Engineering lifecycle designed to maximize data quality, reliability, and business value.

---

# Step 1: Data Extraction

## Collecting Data from Business Systems

The first stage of any Data Engineering project is identifying and accessing the data sources that drive business operations.

Organizations typically store information across multiple platforms, including:

* ERP systems
* CRM platforms
* Accounting software
* E-commerce platforms
* Excel spreadsheets
* CSV files
* Marketing platforms
* Website analytics tools
* Internal databases
* Third-party APIs

While each system may contain valuable information, these sources often operate independently and do not provide a unified view of business performance.

The objective of the extraction phase is to securely collect and centralize data from all relevant business systems.

### Key Activities

* Source system assessment
* Data source inventory creation
* API integrations
* Database connectivity setup
* File ingestion processes
* Scheduled data extraction workflows

### Business Outcome

Data that was previously scattered across multiple systems becomes accessible within a centralized analytics environment.

---

# Step 2: Data Cleaning

## Improving Data Quality and Consistency

Raw business data is rarely ready for analytics.

Data quality issues frequently impact reporting accuracy and decision-making confidence.

Common challenges include:

* Duplicate records
* Missing values
* Inconsistent formats
* Invalid entries
* Data entry errors
* Incomplete records
* Conflicting information across systems

Without proper cleaning, even the most sophisticated dashboards can produce misleading results.

The objective of this stage is to improve data quality and establish consistency across all datasets.

### Key Activities

* Duplicate detection and removal
* Missing value assessment
* Standardization of formats
* Data type correction
* Error correction
* Naming convention normalization
* Data quality profiling

### Business Outcome

Data becomes cleaner, more consistent, and significantly more reliable for analysis.

---

# Step 3: Data Transformation and Integration

## Converting Raw Data into Business Information

Once data has been collected and cleaned, it must be transformed into structures that reflect real business operations.

This is where technical data becomes meaningful business information.

Data from multiple systems is combined, standardized, and enriched using business logic.

For example:

* Customer records may be merged across CRM and sales systems.
* Revenue metrics may be standardized across multiple channels.
* Product information may be unified across operational databases.
* Marketing and sales data may be linked to measure campaign performance.

This stage creates relationships between previously disconnected datasets.

### Key Activities

* Data modeling
* Business rule implementation
* Data enrichment
* Dataset integration
* Key generation
* Metric standardization
* Relationship mapping

### Business Outcome

Data becomes connected, contextualized, and aligned with business objectives.

---

# Step 4: Data Validation

## Establishing Trust and Accuracy

Reliable reporting depends on reliable data.

Before data can be used for Business Intelligence, every transformation must be validated against source systems and business expectations.

Validation ensures that data pipelines produce accurate and trustworthy outputs.

This stage is critical because even small discrepancies can lead to incorrect reporting and poor business decisions.

### Key Activities

* Source-to-target reconciliation
* Record count verification
* Revenue validation
* Duplicate detection checks
* Null value analysis
* Business rule validation
* Exception reporting

### Validation Questions

* Does the warehouse contain all expected records?
* Do revenue totals match source systems?
* Are calculations producing accurate results?
* Are business rules being applied correctly?

### Business Outcome

Decision-makers gain confidence that reports and dashboards are based on accurate and trustworthy information.

---

# Step 5: Analytics Dataset Engineering

## Building Reporting-Ready Data Structures

Raw operational data is rarely optimized for reporting.

Business Intelligence platforms require datasets specifically designed for analytics.

At this stage, cleaned and validated data is organized into structures that support efficient reporting and analysis.

These datasets become the foundation for tools such as:

* Power BI
* Looker Studio
* Tableau
* Excel Reporting
* Executive Dashboards
* KPI Monitoring Systems

### Key Activities

* Fact table design
* Dimension table development
* Star schema implementation
* Analytics data modeling
* KPI dataset preparation
* Reporting layer optimization
* Performance tuning

### Business Outcome

Analysts and reporting tools gain access to structured, analytics-ready datasets that simplify reporting and improve performance.

---

### Business Outcome

Organizations gain a scalable analytics foundation that enables faster reporting, better visibility, and more informed business decisions.
