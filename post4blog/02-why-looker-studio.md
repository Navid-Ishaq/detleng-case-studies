# Why Data Engineering Still Matters

One of the most common misconceptions is that reporting tools create business intelligence.

They do not.

Reporting tools simply visualize information.

The quality of the dashboard depends entirely on the quality of the underlying data.

Without Data Engineering:

* Metrics become inconsistent
* Reports become unreliable
* Business decisions become risky

This is why DeTLeng always focuses on building trusted data foundations before developing reporting solutions.
