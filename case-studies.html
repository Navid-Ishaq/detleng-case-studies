Real Implementations. Practical Learning. Business Value.

The DeTLeng Case Study Repository documents real-world Data Engineering, ETL Development, Analytics Engineering, Data Warehousing, Reporting Automation, and Business Intelligence projects developed through practical implementation and structured problem-solving methodologies.

The DeTLeng Principle

We Don't Just Build Dashboards.

We Engineer the Data That Makes Dashboards Trustworthy.

Reliable reporting begins with reliable data. Every project published in this repository demonstrates the engineering processes required to transform raw business data into trusted analytics-ready assets.

Why This Repository Exists

The objective of the DeTLeng Case Study Repository is to document practical implementation experience, demonstrate real-world project methodologies, and share technical knowledge gained through hands-on execution.

Rather than showcasing only final dashboards or reports, each case study highlights the complete journey from raw data to business value.
