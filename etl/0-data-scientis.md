Yar, agar Data Scientist ko simplest business language mein samjhein, to:

> **Data Engineer data ko trustworthy banata hai.**
>
> **Data Scientist data se future, patterns, aur decisions nikalta hai.**

Data Scientist ke major kaam generally ye hote hain:

### 1. Prediction (Forecasting)

Future estimate karna.

Examples:

* Next month's sales
* Future revenue
* Inventory demand
* Website traffic

Question:

> "Agla mahina kaisa hoga?"

---

### 2. Customer Churn Prediction

Kaun customer business chhorne wala hai?

Examples:

* Subscription cancellations
* Customer inactivity
* Retention risk

Question:

> "Kaun customer khone wale hain?"

---

### 3. Customer Segmentation

Customers ko groups mein divide karna.

Examples:

* High Value Customers
* Medium Value Customers
* At Risk Customers
* New Customers

Question:

> "Hamare customers kis type ke hain?"

---

### 4. Recommendation Systems

Users ko relevant suggestions dena.

Examples:

* Amazon product recommendations
* Netflix movie suggestions
* YouTube video recommendations

Question:

> "User ko kya recommend karna chahiye?"

---

### 5. Anomaly Detection

Unusual behavior identify karna.

Examples:

* Fraud detection
* Sudden revenue drops
* Suspicious transactions
* System failures

Question:

> "Kya kuch abnormal ho raha hai?"

---

### 6. Classification Models

Kisi cheez ko category mein rakhna.

Examples:

* Spam vs Non-Spam
* Fraud vs Legitimate
* High Risk vs Low Risk

Question:

> "Yeh kis category mein aata hai?"

---

### 7. Optimization

Best action identify karna.

Examples:

* Best pricing
* Best marketing budget allocation
* Best delivery routes

Question:

> "Sab se behtar decision kya hai?"

---

### 8. A/B Testing & Experimentation

Changes ka impact measure karna.

Examples:

* New landing page
* New pricing model
* New marketing campaign

Question:

> "Kya naya version purane se better hai?"

---

### 9. Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Text ko analyze karna.

Examples:

* Customer reviews
* Sentiment analysis
* Support tickets
* Chatbots

Question:

> "Log kya keh rahe hain?"

---

### 10. Machine Learning Models

Patterns seekhna aur automated decisions lena.

Examples:

* Lead scoring
* Demand forecasting
* Fraud scoring
* Recommendation engines

Question:

> "System khud seekh kar better decisions kaise le?"

---

## The 3 Questions Framework

### Data Engineer

> What data do we have?

Builds:

* ETL
* BigQuery
* Warehouses
* Analytics Datasets

---

### Data Analyst

> What happened?

Builds:

* Reports
* Dashboards
* KPIs
* Insights

---

### Data Scientist

> What will happen?
>
> Why is it happening?
>
> What should we do next?

Builds:

* Predictions
* Forecasts
* Models
* Optimization Systems

---

For most SMBs and mid-sized companies, the biggest pain is usually still:

> **"Can we trust our numbers?"**

That's why Data Engineering often delivers value sooner than Data Science. Once the data foundation is reliable, Data Science becomes much more effective.
